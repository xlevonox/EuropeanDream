Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game European Dream developed by Levonox (“we”, “us”). Levonox is the data controller for the data described below.

Information We Collect

European Dream has no accounts and does not ask for personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or contacts. It does not show ads.

To keep the game stable and understand how it is played, the app uses these Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics – app interactions (for example screens viewed, game features used, session length), device information (device model, operating system version, language) and an approximate location (country or region, derived by Google from your IP address; the app never uses GPS or location permissions).
  • Firebase Crashlytics – crash reports and diagnostics (device model, operating system version, app version, error logs) so we can fix technical problems.
  • Firebase Remote Config – downloads game settings from Google; it uses a Firebase installation identifier.

These services use identifiers created for this app installation (the Firebase installation ID and the app instance ID). Starting with version 1.1.0, the game does not use the Android advertising ID and Google Analytics data is not used for ad personalization.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with other companies, except Google, which processes it on our behalf as a service provider under the Firebase Data Processing Terms and its Privacy Policy. Google may process this data in the United States and other countries, under the safeguards described in those terms.

Why We Use This Data

We use this data only to find and fix crashes, keep the game working, and understand which parts of the game players use so we can improve it. We do not use it to identify you personally. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing a stable and improving game (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Data Retention

Analytics and crash data are kept only as long as needed for these purposes. Our own exported copies are deleted automatically after 60 days. Google keeps analytics data for at most 14 months and crash reports for about 90 days, according to the Firebase retention settings.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers. Android may include them in your device backup if you have backups enabled. Uninstalling the game deletes them from the device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game can remind you to play. These reminders are scheduled on your device; no data is sent for them. You can turn them off in the game settings or in Android settings.

Other Features

The “Rate this app” button opens Google Play’s review dialog or the Google Play Store page. Any review you write is handled by Google Play. Links in the “Licenses & credits” screen open external websites in your browser.

Your Rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because we do not collect your name or contact details, we may need information from you to find the data linked to your device. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. You can also stop all collection at any time by uninstalling the game.

Deleting Your Data

Wherever you live, you can ask us to delete the data collected through European Dream:

  1. Send an email to xlevonox@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”.
  2. Tell us your device model, your country and roughly when you played, so we can look for the data linked to your device. The game has no accounts, so please do not send passwords or identity documents.

We will reply within 30 days and delete the analytics and crash data we can identify as yours. Data we cannot link to you is deleted automatically after the periods listed under “Data Retention”. Your game progress is stored only on your device, and in your device backup if you have backups enabled; uninstalling the game deletes it from the device.

Children

European Dream is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the game and want their data removed, contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.

Contact

If you have any questions or requests about this privacy policy, please contact us at: xlevonox@gmail.com.

Thank you for playing European Dream.
